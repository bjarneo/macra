# Macra product page

Scope: the one-page GitHub Pages site for Macra. Mode: Persuade.

Audience: people who track calories and macros every day and want a free, private Android app. Action: download the APK from GitHub Releases. Proof: real app screenshots with synthetic demo data, and the plain facts about where data lives. Constraints: no invented users, ratings, or download counts. Copy in ASD-STE100 Simplified Technical English.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The page is the Macra icon at poster scale. An open orange ring closes as the visitor reads, while its center counts the day's kcal down to zero, and it closes at the download. It refuses the category default: a phone mockup hero, three feature cards, and a badge row.

OWN-WORLD: The app's own world. Ground #0C0C0D, surface #18181A, ink #F5F5F2, muted #9B9B97, action red #FF2E4C, ring orange #FF7A1A. Macro colors #88AFFF, #6ED889, #F3BD5C and water #59C8E8 appear only where they mean protein, carbs, fat, and water. Host Grotesk 800 with tight tracking and tabular numerals. Rounded 20 to 28 px surfaces.

STORY: The visitor learns what Macra is, sees the real app at work, learns that the data stays on the phone, and downloads the APK.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Desktop: the open ring fills the left half at about 70vh, with "2,270 kcal left" in its center and the gap at the top right. The right half holds the headline, one sentence, Download APK in red, and View source. Mobile: the ring at full width above the headline, the actions below it.

FORM: The closing ring. Position 5 on the ordered list of seven structures. Seed key 87e53f34. Signature interaction: the ring sweep and the kcal count are tied to scroll progress and complete at the download section. Reduced motion shows the ring static.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
